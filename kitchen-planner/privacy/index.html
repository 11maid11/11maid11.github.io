Privacy Policy

Short version: the app does not collect any data about you.

  • Your kitchens, rooms and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The camera is used only for the live AR preview of your kitchen in your room. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Purchases and the free trial are handled by Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone and iPad). We do not receive your payment details.
  • Reminders before the trial ends are local notifications scheduled on your device, only if you allow them.
  • When you export a PDF or an image, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to share it.

If you delete the app, all its data is deleted from your device.
