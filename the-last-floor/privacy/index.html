Privacy Policy

Short version: we don't collect anything about you. The ads in the game are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads.

  • The game has no account and no sign-in. Your progress, difficulty, language and sound settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch for an extra life. To serve, measure and limit these ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob collects device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, information about your device, and how you interact with the ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings at any time.
  • The "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the game only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone other than Google for the purposes above.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

If you delete the game, all its data is deleted from your device.

  • Igra nema nalog ni prijavu. Napredak, težina, jezik i podešavanja zvuka čuvaju se samo na tvom uređaju.
  • Igra prikazuje reklame preko Google AdMob-a: reklamu preko celog ekrana između nekih nivoa i video reklamu koju možeš sam da izabereš da pogledaš za dodatni život. Da bi prikazao, izmerio i ograničio reklame i sprečio prevaru, AdMob prikuplja identifikatore uređaja (npr. advertising ID), IP adresu i približnu lokaciju iz nje, podatke o uređaju i kako koristiš reklame. Pogledaj kako Google koristi podatke iz aplikacija koje koriste njegove usluge.

Brisanjem igre brišu se i svi njeni podaci sa uređaja.
