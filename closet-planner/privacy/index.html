Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Short version: your designs never leave your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

  • Your designs, rooms and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no account, no advertising and no cross-app tracking.
  • The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to send anonymous usage statistics (for example which screens are opened, whether the trial is started, which plan is chosen, whether an export or the AR preview is used) and Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports (device model, Android version, app version, the error). They are linked to a random app-instance ID, not to your name, e-mail or Google account, and contain no designs, measurements or camera images. The advertising ID is not collected. Google processes this data on our behalf (Firebase privacy). The app has no setting to turn this off; uninstalling the app stops it.
  • The camera is used only for the live AR preview of your furniture in your room. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Google Play account.
  • The reminder before the trial ends is a local notification scheduled on your device, only if you allow notifications.
  • When you export a PDF, an image or a project file, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to share it.

If you delete the app, all its data is deleted from your device.
