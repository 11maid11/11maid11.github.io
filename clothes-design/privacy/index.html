Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Short version: your designs never leave your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

  • Your designs, saved patterns and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Images you add to a design are copied from your gallery into the app and stay on your device.
  • The app has no account, no advertising and no cross-app tracking.
  • The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to send anonymous usage statistics (for example which screens are opened, which garment, colour or pattern is chosen, whether the trial is started, which plan is chosen, whether an export is made) and Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports (device model, Android version, app version, the error). They are linked to a random app-instance ID, not to your name, e-mail or Google account, and contain no designs, texts or images. The advertising ID is not collected. Google processes this data on our behalf (Firebase privacy). The app has no setting to turn this off; uninstalling the app stops it.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by the store you installed the app from (Google Play or Huawei AppGallery). We do not receive your payment details. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your account in that store.
  • When you export images, mockups, a video, a sewing pattern, a print pack or a tech pack, the files are created on your device and shared only where you choose to share them.

If you delete the app, all its data is deleted from your device.
